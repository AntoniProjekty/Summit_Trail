OUR PHILOSOPHY

Craftsmanship built to outlast generations

In our Geneva atelier, master watchmakers blend traditional technique with contemporary design. We use only certified gold, flawless diamonds, and sapphire crystal — for pieces that are more than jewelry: a legacy for the next generation.

OUR STORY

Four decades of precision

1987

The atelier is born

The Laurent family founds a small watchmaking workshop in Geneva, driven by a passion for precise craft.

1998

The Signature Collection debuts

The first flagship boutique opens alongside the collection that still defines the house style today.

2011

Grand Prix d'Horlogerie

The Château Automatik earns recognition from the international watchmaking jury.

2024

A new salon in London

Aurelia opens a private salon in London, bringing European craftsmanship closer to collectors worldwide.

HOW OUR PIECES ARE MADE

From sketch to masterpiece

01

Sketch & Design

Every model begins as a hand-drawn sketch before it reaches the designer's board.

02

Stone Selection

Our gemologists accept fewer than 2% of the stones they examine.

03

Hand Assembly

A watchmaker assembles the movement by hand — often over 80 hours of work on a single piece.

04

Quality Control

Every piece passes a 42-point inspection before leaving the atelier.
